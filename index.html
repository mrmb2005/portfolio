About

I'm a Geoinformatics Engineering student at NUST (IGIS-SCEE) in Islamabad, with hands-on experience from NESPAK and the Center for Water Informatics & Technology at LUMS. I work at the intersection of remote sensing, spatial analysis, and machine learning.

My goal is simple: turn satellite imagery and spatial data into tools people can act on — flood dashboards, air-quality maps, crop classifications, and disaster-risk platforms. I'm especially interested in the defense and security applications of GIS, and I'm always looking for new problems to map.

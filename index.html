Profile / who I am

I'm a Geoinformatics Engineering student at IGIS-SCEE, NUST, with hands-on experience in GIS, remote sensing, and spatial data analysis built through academic and applied projects. I've worked as a GIS Engineer intern at NESPAK, Lahore, and as a Research Intern at the Center for Water Informatics and Technology, LUMS. I'm skilled in satellite image interpretation and web development, and I build tools that turn raw geospatial data into something people can actually act on — flood dashboards, air quality maps, crop classifications, and disaster-risk platforms. I'm especially drawn to defense-related applications, with a growing interest in military GIS.
